Why Buy a Plot on the Indore–Ujjain Road?

The Indore–Ujjain Road links Madhya Pradesh's commercial capital with the temple city of Ujjain. For families who want the space of an independent home without leaving the reach of either city, a residential plot on the Indore–Ujjain Road offers the best of both.

A gated township, not just open land

Unlike scattered roadside plots, Valencia Town at Shahna is a planned, gated township of about 48 acres with nearly 1,000 plots, internal roads, a clubhouse, a temple, children's play areas and forest-style gardens. You build your own home, inside a secure community.

For home buyers and investors from Indore & Ujjain

Whether you are looking for plots in Indore to build your family home, or plots near Ujjain as a long-term investment, Valencia Town gives you a clear, planned layout on one of the region's key corridors.
