A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Questions,
answered.

The essentials, in brief. For anything else, the Valencia team is a conversation away.

Where is Valencia Town located?+

Valencia Town is at Shahna on the Indore–Ujjain Road in Madhya Pradesh, between Indore and Ujjain.

What plot sizes are offered?+

Residential plots are approximately 1,000–2,400 sq ft. Current sizes and availability can be confirmed with the sales team.

What is planned within the community?+

The planned amenities include a clubhouse, temple, children’s playground, forest-style gardens, sports courts, landscaped lawns, senior citizens’ library, meeting zone, women’s activity zone, wide internal roads and gated security. Specifications and layouts are indicative and subject to change.

How well connected is the location?+

Sri Aurobindo Hospital (SAIMS) is approximately 10 minutes away, Vijay Nagar and Indore Airport approximately 25 minutes, and Ujjain approximately 40 minutes. Times vary with traffic and route.

How can I arrange a private visit?+

Use Arrange a Private Visit and leave your name and mobile number. The team can help with a visit, current plot availability or a brochure.

Where can I check approvals and project details?+

Request the current project documentation and applicable approvals from the sales team before making a decision. The communicated project scale is approximately ₹500 crore.

Read more about the location +

Residential plots on the Indore–Ujjain Road

For families exploring residential plots near Indore or Ujjain, Valencia Town brings together planned community spaces and room to build a personal home. Its Shahna address on the Indore–Ujjain corridor connects the township with Sri Aurobindo Hospital (SAIMS), Vijay Nagar and the wider region.

When considering plots on Indore–Ujjain Road, explore the location in person, review current layout documentation and confirm available plot sizes. Our team can help you understand the planned amenities and arrange a private visit.
