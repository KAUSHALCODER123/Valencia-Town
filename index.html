Why the Indore–Ujjain Road

The road connects Indore, the state's commercial centre, with Ujjain. Buying a plot here means room for an independent house without cutting ties to either city.

Most land along the road is sold as open plots. Valencia Town is planned as one gated township, with internal roads, a clubhouse, a temple, play areas and gardens already in the layout. That makes it a fit for families from Indore or Ujjain building a home, and for buyers holding a plot as a long-term investment.
