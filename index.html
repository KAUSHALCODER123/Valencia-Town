Mahakalis calling

you home.

Some come for a darshan. Some stay for a lifetime. Every year, crores of devotees travel to Ujjain; at Valencia Town the dawn Bhasma Aarti is a morning drive.

A day, forty minutes from darshan

  1. 4 amBhasma Aarti at Shri Mahakaleshwar, the only one of its kind among the twelve Jyotirlingas.
  2. ~5:30 amThe drive home along the Indore–Ujjain Road, about forty minutes.
  3. EveningBack for the evening aarti, and home in time for dinner.
