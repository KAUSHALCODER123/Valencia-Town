Why buy a plot on the Indore–Ujjain Road?

The Indore–Ujjain Road links Madhya Pradesh's commercial capital with the temple city of Ujjain. For families who want the space of an independent home within reach of both cities, a residential plot on this road offers the best of both.

Unlike scattered roadside plots, Valencia Town at Shahna is a planned, gated township of about 48 acres with nearly 1,000 plots, internal roads, a clubhouse, a temple, children's play areas and forest-style gardens.

Whether you are looking for plots in Indore to build a family home, or plots near Ujjain as a long-term investment, Valencia Town offers a clear, planned layout on one of the region's key corridors.

Where is Valencia Town located?

Valencia Town is at Shahna on the Indore–Ujjain Road, Madhya Pradesh: about 10 minutes from Sri Aurobindo Hospital, 25 minutes from Vijay Nagar and Indore Airport, and 40 minutes from Ujjain.

What plot sizes are available?

Plots range from 1,000 to 2,400 sq ft. Ask for the price list to see the sizes currently available.

How big is Valencia Town?

The township is spread across approximately 48 acres with nearly 1,000 plots, representing an investment of around ₹500 crore.

What amenities does Valencia Town offer?

A clubhouse, temple, children's playground, library for senior citizens, meeting zone, women's activity zone, forest-style gardens, a dense forest zone and a gated, secure campus.

Is Valencia Town a gated community?

Yes. Valencia Town is a complete gated and secure community campus designed for every generation of the family.

Are there residential plots for sale on the Indore–Ujjain Road?

Yes. Valencia Town offers residential plots in a 48-acre gated township at Shahna on the Indore–Ujjain Road, between Indore and Ujjain.

Is Valencia Town good for buyers from both Indore and Ujjain?

Yes. It is about 25 minutes from Vijay Nagar in Indore and 40 minutes from Ujjain, so families working or living in either city can reach it easily.
