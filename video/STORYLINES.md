# Valencia Town: film storylines

Facts used (all from the site's own copy, `data/project.json` and `content/en.json`): 48 acres, 1,000+ plots of about 1,000–2,400 sq ft, Shahna on the Indore–Ujjain Road, 40 min to Mahakaleshwar, 25 min to Vijay Nagar and Indore Airport, 10 min to Sri Aurobindo Hospital, clubhouse, temple, forest gardens, sports courts, children's play areas, wide internal roads, gated township. Picture source: the project's own renders in `assets/img/` (artist's impressions, labelled as such). No price, phone, RERA number or developer name appears, because none is confirmed yet.

Common: 1920×1080, 30 fps, about 30–35 s, music-only (free local music), end card shows valenciatown.in and "Book a site visit".

## A. The Dawn Drive (recommended)
**Device:** a moment in someone's day. **Form:** one continuous take along the road.
The viewer sees: 4 am, a dark road and a time stamp, "Bhasma Aarti, Ujjain". As the sky warms to dawn the camera drives the Indore–Ujjain Road home and arrives at the Valencia gate at sunrise, then travels in through the arch walk, gardens, clubhouse and play area.
First two seconds: black-green night road, "4:00 AM" stamp, the first road lights wiping on.
Beats: hook (4 am, darshan) → tension (the long drive home, the km counter) → reveal (gate and name) → proof (40 min / 25 min / 10 min as stops on the route) → payoff (the aerial masterplan opens, 48 acres) → claim ("Mahakal is calling you home.") → end card.
Look: night green and ink to dawn amber, Cormorant Garamond caps, one amber route line that stays on screen. Sound: slow tanpura-like pad rising to a warm piano, soft bells at the gate.

## B. Forty-Eight Acres, Drawn
**Device:** the object is the explanation. **Form:** an endless zoom into the masterplan.
The viewer sees the masterplan from above, then the camera dives into one zone, which becomes the render of that place (temple, clubhouse, play area, sports court), then out to the next zone, until the whole township has been walked.
First two seconds: the plan already drawing itself in amber lines over dark ground.
Beats: hook (plan draws) → reveal (name) → proof (4 zones, one dive each, one real line of copy each) → payoff (pull back to all 48 acres) → claim → end card.
Look: ink ground, hairline amber drawing, bone-white captions. Sound: minimal pulse, clicks as zones lock.

## C. One Number Per Beat (the bold one)
**Device:** a number lands. **Form:** cut on the beat, giant kinetic type.
Giant type cuts on the music: 48, 40, 25, 10, 1,000+, each number flipping into the render that proves it (acres into aerial, 40 into the Ujjain route, 10 into the hospital run) before the next hits.
First two seconds: "48" fills the frame and tears open onto the aerial.
Beats: hook (48) → proof (four numbers, four images) → payoff (1,000+ plots, the full gallery in a fast grid) → claim → end card.
Look: bone and amber colour fields, heavy serif numerals. Sound: percussive, cut on the beat.

## Assumptions
Length 30–35 s, 16:9, no voiceover or captions, music-only using free local tracks (no paid HeyGen music or voice).
