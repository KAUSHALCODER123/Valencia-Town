A morning darshan, home by breakfast

The Bhasma Aarti at Shri Mahakaleshwar is held before dawn, around 4 am. From Valencia Town it is a drive of about 40 minutes each way, so a darshan no longer means a journey. Aarti timings and bookings are set by the temple; check its official website before you go.

Shravan Mondays, Mahashivratri and Nag Panchami are within reach every year, not once in a lifetime.

Simhastha 2028

Simhastha, the Kumbh of Ujjain held once in twelve years, takes place in 2028, and the Indore–Ujjain Road widening is timed with it. Any effect on property prices is uncertain; treat it as context, not a promise of returns.

Near Ujjain, with Indore close by

Living between the two cities keeps Indore’s everyday needs near: Sri Aurobindo Hospital about 10 minutes, Vijay Nagar and Indore Airport about 25 minutes. Inside the 48-acre township there is a temple of its own, alongside the clubhouse, gardens, sports courts and play areas.
